Technical field immersion

Barrage M'dez - electrical automation

In July 2025, I spent several days at Barrage M'dez to understand how a large hydraulic structure is operated from an electrical and automation perspective. The visit gave me direct exposure to the dam's electrical cabinets, protection devices, instrumentation, power distribution and the automation logic behind gates, valves and hydraulic equipment. A key moment was meeting the automation team while they were programming the PLC system that will supervise and coordinate the dam's operating sequences, alarms, interlocks and field commands.

Hydraulic infrastructure PLC programming Gates and valves Electrical cabinets Supervision
01 PLC architecture

Observed how the future control system is structured around inputs, outputs, sequences, alarms and supervised commands.

02 Actuated organs

Linked automation logic with gates, valves, motors, limit switches and safety conditions required for hydraulic operation.

03 Electrical layer

Studied cabinets, protections, relays, distribution circuits, wiring organization and the role of auxiliary power.

04 Operational safety

Understood why dam automation requires conservative logic, manual fallback, clear diagnostics and disciplined field validation.

Academic field visit

Centrale thermique de Mohammedia - ONEE-BE

In May 2025, I carried out an academic field visit to the Mohammedia thermal power plant. The visit helped me connect classroom engineering with a real generation site: operating modes, production units, field teams, control-command architecture, supervision and the electrical path from generation to grid interface.

Thermal generation Control room Electrical systems Automation Operations
01 Plant operation

Discovered the site organization, main production units, auxiliary systems and the way operating modes are coordinated.

02 Automation layer

Observed how control-command, supervision, alarms and operator interfaces support real-time industrial decisions.

03 Electrical chain

Linked thermal production with alternators, transformers, protections, distribution logic and grid-oriented operation.

04 People and missions

Understood the roles of operators, electrical teams, maintenance staff and safety discipline in plant reliability.

Industrial field visit

Nexans - cable manufacturing

In December 2024, I visited a Nexans production site dedicated to electrical cables. The experience gave me a practical view of how cable reliability is built across production: conductor preparation, reel handling, insulation and sheathing processes, line monitoring, quality checks, traceability, safety discipline and the coordination between operators, maintenance teams and quality staff.

Electrical cables Production lines Quality control HSE culture Industrial automation
01 Cable process

Followed the industrial route from conductor handling to cable forming, insulation, sheathing, marking and final preparation.

02 Line control

Observed how motors, drives, sensors, tension control, line speed and operator interfaces keep production stable.

03 Quality mindset

Learned how process parameters, visual inspection, electrical tests and traceability protect cable performance and reliability.

04 Safety culture

Connected PPE, machine guarding, LOTO logic, reel movement and golden rules with the reality of daily industrial work.

Academic field visit

Groupe Cosumar - sugar process

In November 2023, I carried out an academic visit to a Groupe Cosumar industrial site. Cosumar is Morocco's historic sugar producer, active in sugar production from sugar plants and the refining of raw sugar. During the visit, we discovered the sugar beet treatment route, the main stages of sugar production, the plant's automation layer and the control-command logic used to supervise process continuity, quality and safety.

Sugar beet Process industry Control-command Automation Quality
01 Process chain

Followed the logic of beet reception, preparation, extraction, purification, evaporation, crystallization and conditioning.

02 Control layer

Observed how sensors, actuators, valves, drives, setpoints, alarms and supervision support continuous production.

03 Quality logic

Connected process parameters, laboratory checks, cleanliness, traceability and product standards with final sugar quality.

04 Plant rhythm

Understood how operators, maintenance teams, utilities and safety routines keep a large food-process plant stable.
